## If Preview Doesn't Show Immediately

**Don't worry!** Platforms cache link previews. Try:

1. **Clear Cache on Platform:**
   - Use debugging tools (links above)
   - Click "Scrape Again" or "Refresh"

2. **Wait 24-48 Hours:**
   - Some platforms take time to update cache
   - Check again after a day

3. **Clear App Cache:**
   - WhatsApp: Settings > Storage > Clear Cache
   - Then share link again
